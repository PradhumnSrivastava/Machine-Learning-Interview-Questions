# Questions Inbox
1. What is Data Science? Ans- Data Science is an interdisciplinary field that uses statistics, mathematics, programming, and machine learning to extract meaningful insights from data and support decision-making.

2. What is the difference between Data Science and Data Analytics? Ans- Data Science involves building predictive models, developing algorithms, and extracting insights from data, while Data Analytics primarily focuses on examining existing data to identify trends and support business decisions.

3. Why is Data Science becoming increasingly popular? Ans- Data Science is growing in popularity because organizations generate massive amounts of data and need data-driven insights for automation, prediction, personalization, and better decision-making.

4. What are the main components of Data Science? Ans- The main components include data collection, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, statistics, feature engineering, machine learning, model evaluation, and communicating insights.

5. What are the different types of analytics used in Data Science? Ans- The four major types are descriptive analytics, which explains what happened; diagnostic analytics, which explains why it happened; predictive analytics, which forecasts what may happen; and prescriptive analytics, which recommends what action to take.

6. What is the role of statistics in Data Science? Ans- Statistics helps summarize data, identify patterns, measure uncertainty, test hypotheses, understand relationships between variables, and evaluate the reliability of conclusions.

7. What is the difference between structured and unstructured data? Ans- Structured data follows a predefined format, such as rows and columns in a database, while unstructured data lacks a fixed tabular structure, such as images, videos, audio, and free-text documents.

8. What are the major steps involved in a Data Science project? Ans- A typical project involves problem definition, data collection, data cleaning, exploratory data analysis, feature engineering, model development, evaluation, deployment, and monitoring.

9. How is Data Science used in business? Ans- Businesses use Data Science for customer segmentation, sales forecasting, fraud detection, recommendation systems, demand prediction, risk assessment, and improving operational efficiency.

10. What is the difference between Business Intelligence (BI) and Data Science? Ans- Business Intelligence mainly uses reports, dashboards, and historical data to understand business performance, while Data Science also applies statistical and machine learning techniques to discover patterns and make predictions.
